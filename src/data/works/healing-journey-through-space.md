## Frameworks and tools

- Figma: the design and the clickable prototype, made for the XD4 assignment.
- Research methods: ideation, an early prototype, and testing with iterations.

## Features

- A home page that introduces the generational spaceship and the challenge
- Pages for the ideation phase, the early prototype, and testing and iterations
- A calm style with a soft cream, orange and pink gradient and a mix of serif and sans-serif type

## What I learned

Designing for a situation that doesn't exist yet made us base every decision on research, not on what we already knew. Testing early prototypes showed us which ideas actually helped and which ones only looked good.
