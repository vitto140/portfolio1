## Frameworks and tools

- HTML: the structure of the home, tips and contact pages.
- CSS: the dark theme, the glowing gradient and the typewriter-style heading.

## Features

- A home page with a large "Exit Your Matrix" heading and a Call button
- Navigation to the home, tips and contact pages
- An illustration of a pill bottle with a pixel dinosaur over an orange and purple glow

## What I learned

A dark theme with one strong glow gave the page its mood without needing many images. I also learned how much letter spacing and font choice change the tone of a heading.
