## Frameworks and tools

- Figma: the design and the clickable prototype of the site.
- User journey map: the steps a donor goes through, from the first visit to the donation.

## Features

- A bold red and white style that matches the Red Cross brand
- Sections for the intro, the team members, the user journey map, the pain points and opportunities, and our solution
- A Start the journey button that leads the visitor through the story

## What I learned

Mapping the journey step by step showed us where donors drop out, and that gave our solution a clear reason to exist. I also learned to keep a strong visual style without losing the structure of the content.
