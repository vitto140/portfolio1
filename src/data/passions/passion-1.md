## Why I love it
I grew up watching my father work in the kitchen. Cooking is how my family shows care, and making a dish from scratch, tasting and adjusting until it's right, is one of the most satisfying things I know.
## How it shows up in my work
Cooking taught me to prepare everything before I start and to clean up as I go. I work the same way in code: plan first, keep commits small, and taste test often in the browser.
