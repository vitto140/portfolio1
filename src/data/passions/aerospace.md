## Why I love it
Every flight still feels a bit like magic to me. I love looking down at the world from above and thinking about how much engineering it takes to keep something that heavy in the air.
## How it shows up in my work
Aerospace is all about precision and checking everything twice. I try to bring that to my sites by testing at every screen size before I call something done.
